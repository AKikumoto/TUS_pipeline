## Naming convention

```
{structure}_{source}[_{parameter}]_mask_MNI.nii.gz
```

**Masks are bilateral. Side is not in the name**, `run_reg.py` (step 3) splits
them into `_L` / `_R` in native space, and everything downstream selects with
`--side`. A mask carrying `left`/`right`/`_L` in its own name meant the side was
tracked in two places at once, and produced filenames like
`aHipp_Weizhen_p05_L`.

**No construction expressions.** `rHipp_BN` recorded how step 02
built the mask (a boolean OR of two atlas parcels); that belongs in this
document, not in the filename. It is `rHipp_BN`.

**Source is always present**, so a mask can be traced without opening this file:
`BN` Brainnetome, `CIT168`, `Maass2015`, `Blackford2022`, `Dahl2022`,
`NeuroSynth112`, `Weizhen`, `sphere8` (8 mm coordinate sphere).

**Parameter** carries a threshold or variant when one exists, `_p01`, `_p05`
are significance levels, not probabilities, so p05 is the *larger* mask
(763 voxels against 417).

### Exception: left-only masks

`aHipp_Weizhen_*` exists for the left hemisphere only; there is no right-side
source. It therefore cannot follow the bilateral rule, and **step 3's L/R split
will produce an empty `_R`**. Use it with `--side _L` or `--side ""`.

### `aux/`

Not targets. `MNI_hemispheres_BN_1mm.nii.gz` (the hemisphere map, which has
parcellation coverage holes, see `run_reg.py`), `aMCC_bounding_box`, and
`aux/superseded/` holding the pre-merge EC left/right masks.

## 10. Entorhinal cortex: Maass et al. 2015 functional subdivision

> **Merged 2026-08-21.** Maass supplies left and right separately; they are
> now combined into one bilateral mask each, split by step 3 like every
> other target. The two sides do not touch, 22–28 mm apart, nothing inside
> |x| < 1 mm, so the x-sign split reproduces the originals exactly
> (1185 + 1125 and 441 + 507). The pre-merge files are in
> `aux/superseded/`. Do **not** split these with the Brainnetome hemisphere
> map: it has coverage holes over entorhinal cortex and drops 18–20 %.

Anterolateral (alEC) and posteromedial (pmEC) entorhinal cortex, defined by
resting-state connectivity preference: alEC couples with perirhinal cortex
(PRC), pmEC with parahippocampal cortex (PHC). This is the standard functional
parcellation of human EC, and it is what "anterior vs posterior EC" refers to:
the axis is anterolateral↔posteromedial, not a straight A–P cut.

The two are 7 mm apart in y and 5 mm in z. **pmEC is less than half the size of
alEC** (441 vs 1185 voxels on the left), which matters for TUS: the −3 dB focal
lobe measured on this pipeline is ~5 mm across laterally and 45–55 mm long
axially, so pmEC is small relative to the axial extent.

## 11. Hippocampus: Weizhen masks

**Kept separate from the Brainnetome hippocampus above; the two are not
interchangeable.** Different atlas, different extent, different laterality: the
Weizhen anterior mask is left-only and 763 voxels, the Brainnetome rHipp is
bilateral and 8579. Do not pair a Weizhen anterior mask with a Brainnetome
caudal mask as an anterior/posterior contrast, for that, use rHipp and cHipp,
which come from the same parcellation.

The `0.01` / `0.05` suffix is the threshold applied when the mask was made;
`0.05` is the more permissive and therefore the larger.
